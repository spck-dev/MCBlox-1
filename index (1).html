Why MCBlox is useful?

MCBlox is made to help Minecraft players learn commands, command blocks, and advanced creations easily. Our goal is to turn ideas into reality by teaching the tools needed to build amazing Minecraft projects.

What is a Command Block?

A Command Block is a special block that automatically runs commands. It lets you teleport players, give items, change the weather and much more.

How to get a Command Block?

Command Blocks cannot be crafted or found in Survival mode. Enable cheats and run:

/give @s command_block
